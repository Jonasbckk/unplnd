UNPLND Product Vision

Version 1.0

Why UNPLND Exists

Every weekend.
Every evening.
Every vacation.
Millions of people ask the exact same question.

          "What should we do today?"
Not because there aren't enough options.
But because there are too many.
People open Google Maps.
TikTok.
Instagram.
Reddit.
Tripadvisor.
Google.
Weather.
Events.
Restaurant Apps.
Friends Chat.
After 30 minutes…

They still have no plan.
UNPLND exists to eliminate this friction forever.

The Problem

The biggest problem is not finding places.
The biggest problem is making decisions.
Decision fatigue destroys spontaneity.
People don't want 500 restaurants.
People want ONE amazing evening.

Our Promise

Open UNPLND.
Answer a few simple questions.
Receive a complete experience.
Done.
No stress.
No endless scrolling.
No discussions.
No planning.
Just memories.

Vision Statement

UNPLND becomes the operating system for free time.
Whenever someone has free time—
UNPLND is the first app they open.

Long-Term Vision

UNPLND should become the place where life's best memories begin.
The app should not only help people decide what to do.
It should become part of the memory itself.
Years later, users should open their old albums and remember:

        • first dates
        • birthdays
        • spontaneous road trips
        • holidays
        • celebrations
        • random Tuesdays that became unforgettable
UNPLND is not about planning.
UNPLND is about remembering.

Product Principles

Every feature must answer YES to at least one of these questions:
Does it reduce decision fatigue?
Does it create better memories?
Does it increase spontaneity?
Does it strengthen friendships?
Does it make the experience feel magical?
If not—
the feature does not belong in UNPLND.

Golden Rule

People won't remember the AI.
They won't remember the interface.
