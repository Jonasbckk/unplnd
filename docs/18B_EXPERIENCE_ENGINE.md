UNPLND Experience Engine

Version 1.0
Priority: CRITICAL
Status: MVP

Purpose

UNPLND does not generate routes.
UNPLND creates experiences.
The difference is everything.
A perfect evening is not a list of places.
It is a story.
Every generated evening should have:
A beginning.
A middle.
A climax.
A satisfying ending.

Experience Philosophy

People rarely remember:
The restaurant.
The coffee.
The parking lot.
People remember:

The sunset.
The conversation.
The laugh.
The surprise.
The feeling.
The Experience Engine exists to maximize memories.

Rule 1

Every stop needs a reason.
Never include a location because it is popular.
Ask:
Why is this stop here?
If no answer exists...
Remove it.

Rule 2

Avoid Decision Fatigue
Users should never choose between:
15 restaurants
28 cafés
42 bars
UNPLND decides.
Confidence beats quantity.

Rule 3

Quality over Quantity
Three unforgettable places
↓
Better than
Seven average places.
Never maximize stop count.

                                                                         3
Maximize experience quality.

Rule 4

Build Anticipation
Every next stop should increase curiosity.
Users should think:
"I'm excited for what's next."

Rule 5

Respect Energy
Not every evening should become louder.
Some evenings should become calmer.
The engine must understand pacing.
