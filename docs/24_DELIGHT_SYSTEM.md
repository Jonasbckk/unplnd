UNPLND Delight System

Version 1.0
Priority: HIGH
Status: MVP

Purpose

Great products solve problems.
Exceptional products create emotions.
The Delight System defines every tiny moment that makes UNPLND feel alive.
These moments are never required.
But together they create love for the product.

Philosophy

Users should smile.
Often.
Unexpectedly.
Without the app ever becoming childish.
Delight should feel mature.
Premium.
Human.
Never gimmicky.

                                                                         9
DO NOT

Do NOT use confetti.
Do NOT use flashing animations.
Do NOT spam celebrations.
Do NOT imitate mobile games.
Do NOT use fake urgency.
Delight should always feel earned.

Success Criteria

Users occasionally say:
"I don't know why...
but I love opening this app."
That is success.

Golden Rule

Delight is invisible.
People won't remember the animation.
They'll remember how the app made them feel.

Claude Instructions

Whenever you implement a feature,
ask yourself:
Where is the tiny moment of joy?
If none exists,
