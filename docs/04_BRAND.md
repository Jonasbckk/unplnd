UNPLND Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0

Brand Identity

UNPLND is not a technology company.
UNPLND is a lifestyle brand.
Technology is invisible.
Experiences are visible.
The AI is not the product.
The evening is the product.

Brand Mission

Help people spend less time planning.
Help people spend more time living.
Every recommendation should become a memory.

Logo Rules

The logo must always feel premium.
Never stretch it.
Never rotate it.
Never use gradients inside the wordmark.
Never add shadows.
Never use outlines.
Always leave generous spacing.
The app icon should always be recognizable at 32px.

App Icon

Simple.
Rounded.
Minimal.
Recognizable.
One symbol.
No text.
Works in dark mode.
Works in light mode.
Works on iOS and Android.

Social Media

UNPLND does not create content about technology.
UNPLND creates content about experiences.
Good examples:
"AI planned our perfect first date."
"Can AI save a boring Saturday?"
"We gave AI €20 to plan our night."
Bad examples:
"Today we built Firebase."
"We fixed a bug."
"We updated our backend."
Nobody outside developers cares.

TikTok Strategy

Hook with the problem.
Show the experience.
Reveal the app.
End with curiosity.
The video is about the evening.
Not the app.

Community

Users should feel like they belong.

                                                                         8
Encourage sharing.
Encourage inviting friends.
Celebrate memories.
Celebrate spontaneity.

Premium Feeling

Premium is not expensive.
Premium is attention to detail.
Perfect spacing.
Perfect animations.
Perfect typography.
Everything should feel intentional.

Accessibility

Premium products are usable by everyone.
Accessibility is part of the brand.
Not an afterthought.

Brand Test

If you remove the logo...
Could someone still recognize this as UNPLND?
If not...
The branding is not strong enough.

                                                                         9
Final Principle

UNPLND should never feel like software.
It should feel like the beginning of a great story.

Claude Instructions

Whenever you build a screen...
Ask yourself:
Does this feel warm?
Does this feel premium?
Does this feel effortless?
Would someone enjoy looking at this for five minutes?
If not...
Improve it.
Never settle for average.
