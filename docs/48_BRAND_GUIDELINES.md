UNPLND Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0
Priority: CRITICAL
Status: Foundation

Purpose

This document defines the identity of UNPLND.
Not the logo.
Not the colors.
Not the app.
The brand.
Every future decision must align with this document.

Brand Essence

UNPLND exists because the best moments in life are rarely planned.
Sometimes all people need is a little inspiration to create unforgettable memories.
UNPLND helps turn ordinary evenings into stories worth remembering.

Mission

Help people spend less time deciding
and more time living.

                                                                         1
Vision

Become the world's favorite app for creating real-life experiences.
Not by keeping people inside the app,
but by helping them enjoy life outside of it.

Brand Promise

Every time you open UNPLND,
you should feel closer to your next favorite memory.

Core Values

Real life first.
Memories over metrics.
Quality over quantity.
Trust over growth.
Warmth over perfection.
Long-term thinking.
Simplicity.
Curiosity.
Authenticity.

Brand Personality

If UNPLND were a person:
Curious.
Reliable.

                                                                         2
Calm.
Optimistic.
Creative.
Thoughtful.
Never loud.
Never arrogant.
Never trendy just to be trendy.

What UNPLND Is

An experience companion.
A memory creator.
A source of inspiration.
A planning assistant.
A trusted guide.

What UNPLND Is NOT

A social media app.
A booking platform.
A food delivery app.
A review platform.
A travel agency.
A map replacement.
An AI chatbot.

Competitive Position

Others help users consume content.
UNPLND helps users create memories.

Success Definition

Success is not:
More screen time.
More notifications.
More clicks.
Success is:
More evenings together.
More laughter.
More adventures.
More memories.

Partnerships

Only partner with brands that improve experiences.
Never sacrifice trust for revenue.
Never recommend something purely because it pays more.

Marketing Principles

Show real people.
Show real evenings.
Show real emotions.
Never overpromise.
Never fake authenticity.

Future Vision

Ten years from now,
people should associate UNPLND with:
Adventure.
Spontaneity.
Memories.
Friendship.
Warm evenings.
Life outside the screen.

Internal Motto

Plan less.

Live more.

Delight Checklist

✓ Feels human.
✓ Feels timeless.
✓ Feels trustworthy.
✓ Feels optimistic.
✓ Feels premium.

DO NOT

Do NOT become another social network.
Do NOT optimize for addiction.
Do NOT chase trends.
Do NOT sacrifice trust.
Do NOT compromise the mission for growth.

Golden Rule

Every product decision should answer:
Does this help people create meaningful real-world memories?
If not,
it does not belong in UNPLND.

Claude Instructions

When implementing any feature,
protect the brand before the feature.
