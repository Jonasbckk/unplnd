UNPLND Launch & App Store Strategy

Version 1.0
Priority: CRITICAL
Status: Launch

Purpose

This document defines how UNPLND launches to the public.
The launch is not a single day.
It is a carefully planned process.
Every first impression matters.

Launch Philosophy

Do not launch when the app is finished.
Launch when the core experience feels unforgettable.
Quality creates retention.
Retention creates growth.

Launch Goals

Primary Goal
Create delighted users.
Secondary Goal
Collect meaningful feedback.

Golden Rule

A successful launch is not when thousands download the app.
A successful launch is when hundreds genuinely love it.
Everything else grows from that.

Final Message

Never build features to impress investors.
Build experiences that impress users.
If users truly love UNPLND,
growth will follow.
Protect the mission.
Protect the memories.
Protect the people behind them.
