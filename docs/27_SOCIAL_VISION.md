Social Experience Vision

Version 1.0
Priority: Future (V2+)
Status: Roadmap

Purpose

Social features should strengthen real-world relationships.
Never replace them.
UNPLND exists to help people spend time together.
Not to spend more time inside the app.

Philosophy

Social is an enhancement.
Not the product.
Planning together.
Experiencing together.
Remembering together.
That is the goal.

Social Principles

Real-world first.
Screen time second.
Privacy first.

DO NOT

Do NOT create follower counts.
Do NOT create influencers.
Do NOT create endless feeds.
Do NOT reward popularity.
Do NOT optimize for screen time.
Do NOT imitate Instagram.
Do NOT imitate Snapchat.

Success Criteria

Success is measured by:
More evenings together.
More memories together.
More invitations accepted.
More shared experiences.
Not by:
Daily screen time.

Golden Rule

Social should bring people together.
Not keep them apart.
If users spend less time chatting because they are already meeting,
UNPLND is succeeding.

Claude Instructions

Every social feature must encourage users to leave their phones behind.
If a feature increases screen time without improving real-life experiences,
it does not belong in UNPLND.
Protect the mission above all else.
