Discover Specification

Version 1.0
Priority: HIGH
Status: MVP

Purpose

Discover exists to inspire.
Not to entertain endlessly.
Users should leave Discover with one idea.
Not after scrolling through one hundred.
The goal is inspiration.
Not attention.

Philosophy

Curated.
Intentional.
Beautiful.
Minimal.
Discover should feel like walking through a boutique.
Not through a supermarket.

Primary Goal

Help users discover experiences they didn't know they wanted.

                                                                         9
Delight Checklist

✓ Beautiful imagery.
✓ Calm browsing.
✓ Inspiring recommendations.
✓ No endless scrolling.
✓ Every collection feels curated.
✓ Easy to save ideas.

DO NOT

Do NOT build an infinite feed.
Do NOT autoplay videos.
Do NOT optimize for screen time.
Do NOT overload users with hundreds of options.
Do NOT imitate TikTok.
Do NOT imitate Instagram Explore.
The goal is inspiration,
not addiction.

Success Criteria

Users should leave Discover thinking:
"I know exactly what I want to do."
Not:
"I'll keep scrolling."
The best Discover session is often a short one.

                                                                        10
Golden Rule

Inspiration should lead to action.
Not endless browsing.

Claude Instructions

Every Discover element should encourage users to experience the real world.
The app should never compete with real life.
The purpose of Discover is to help users close the app,
go outside,
and create memories.
