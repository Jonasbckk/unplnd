Why We Started

UNPLND was created because too many evenings are lost before they even begin.
People spend hours asking:
"What should we do?"
Where should we eat?
Is there anything open?
Should we stay home?
The decision becomes more exhausting than the evening itself.
We believe technology should remove this friction.
Not create more of it.

Our Mission

Help people create more real-world memories.
Not more screen time.
Every feature should make it easier to leave the phone behind and enjoy life.

                                                                         1
Our Vision

To become the world's most loved experience-planning platform.
Not because we have the most features.
But because we help people spend more meaningful time together.

What Success Means

Success is not measured by:
Hours spent in the app.
Notifications opened.
Endless scrolling.
Instead, success means:
Millions of evenings enjoyed.
Millions of memories created.
Millions of conversations that happened because UNPLND made them easier.

What We Refuse To Become

We will never become:
An attention company.
An addictive social network.
A platform built around vanity metrics.
A company that sacrifices trust for growth.

Decisions We Will Always Make

When faced with two options,

                                                                         2
we choose the one that benefits the user,
even if it grows more slowly.
Long-term trust beats short-term revenue.
Always.

Product Philosophy

Technology should disappear.
The experience should remain.
Users should remember:
The sunset.
The laughter.
The conversation.
Not the app.

Company Culture

Build with care.
Move thoughtfully.
Ship quality.
Respect users.
Listen more than we speak.
Stay curious.
Stay humble.

Leadership Principles

Be transparent.

                                                                         3
Admit mistakes.
Protect the team.
Protect the mission.
Celebrate learning.
Never stop improving.

Design Principles

Calm.
Warm.
Timeless.
Human.
Elegant.
Never trendy for the sake of trends.

Engineering Principles

Readable code.
Maintainable architecture.
Long-term thinking.
Security first.
Performance first.
User experience always.

AI Philosophy

AI is a tool.
Never the product.

                                                                         4
If better technology appears,
we will adopt it.
Our identity is not tied to a model.
It is tied to helping people create better evenings.

Privacy Philosophy

Users trust us with memories.
That trust is sacred.
We will never sell personal data.
We will always be transparent.
Privacy is part of the product.

Growth Philosophy

We prefer:
Happy users telling friends
over
Aggressive marketing.
Word of mouth is earned.
Never bought.

Hiring Philosophy

Hire kind people.
Hire curious people.
Hire builders.
Hire people who believe in the mission.

                                                                         5
Skills can be learned.
Character is harder to teach.

Partnership Philosophy

We work only with partners who improve the experience.
Never partner with businesses that damage trust.
Never recommend something purely because it pays more.

Ten-Year Vision

In ten years we hope people say:
"Some of my favorite memories started with UNPLND."
Not:
"I spent so much time using that app."

Internal Motto

Build memories.
Not metrics.

Final Promise

If we ever have to choose between:
More revenue
or
A better experience
We choose the better experience.
Because that is what makes great companies last.

remember why UNPLND exists.
