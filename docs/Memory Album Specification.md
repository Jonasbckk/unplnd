Memory Album Specification

Version 1.0
Priority: CRITICAL
Status: MVP

Purpose

The Memory Album is the emotional reward for completing an experience.
Planning creates anticipation.
The evening creates memories.
The album preserves them.
The album should feel like opening an old photo box years later.

Philosophy

Do not build a photo gallery.
Build a memory.
Photos are only one part of the experience.
The album tells the story of the evening.

DO NOT

Do NOT make sharing public by default.

Success Criteria

A user opens an album from two years ago.
Within seconds they remember:
The conversations.
The weather.
The feeling.
The laughter.
Not just the places.
That is the purpose of the Memory Album.

Golden Rule

People don't collect photos.
They collect moments.
The Memory Album exists to preserve those moments beautifully.

Claude Instructions

Every album should feel handcrafted.
Warm.
Personal.
Timeless.
When users open an old album,
they should smile before they even scroll.
