UNPLND Monetization Strategy

Version 1.0
Priority: HIGH
Status: MVP

Purpose

Monetization should support the product.
Never define the product.
Users should happily pay because UNPLND creates value.
Not because they are forced.

Philosophy

Trust is more valuable than short-term revenue.
A user who loves UNPLND is worth more than a user who was pressured into subscribing.
Every monetization decision must answer:
"Does this still feel fair?"
If not,
do not implement it.

                                                                         3
No confusing tiers.

Transparent billing.
Monthly
Yearly
Annual plan encouraged with fair savings.

Free Limits

Only soft limits.
Example
5 AI plans per week.
Afterwards
Wait
Or upgrade.
Albums remain unlimited.
Viewing old memories remains unlimited.
Never lock memories.

Paywall

Appears only when value has been demonstrated.
Never on first launch.
Never before first successful evening.
Users should understand Premium before seeing pricing.

Partner Content

Clearly labeled.
Never disguised as organic recommendations.
Sponsored experiences must still meet quality standards.

Affiliate Links

Allowed only when they genuinely improve the experience.
Examples
Restaurant booking
Event tickets
Hotel booking
Activity reservations
Never recommend lower-quality experiences because of commission.

Advertising

No banner ads.
No fullscreen ads.
No rewarded ads.
No autoplay ads.
Advertising destroys trust.

DO NOT

Do NOT sell user data.
Do NOT sell precise location history.
Do NOT display intrusive ads.
Do NOT prioritize sponsored content over quality.
Do NOT lock users out of their memories.
Do NOT paywall the core experience.

Golden Rule

UNPLND earns money because users love it.
Not because users have no choice.
